The problem

The data is there. What is missing is turning it into action.

Fertigation, irrigation and climate are still largely managed by experience, or with systems that stop at monitoring. Decision support software makes suggestions, but someone still has to act by hand.

With fertiliser and energy costs rising and fewer skilled staff available, every late correction costs yield and margin.

~70%of global freshwater withdrawals go to agriculture.FAO, 2023
>20%expected annual growth for controlled environment agriculture: high-tech greenhouses, soilless systems and vertical farms.CAGR · Grand View Research, 2024
Every hourthe parameters of a soilless crop change. A system that only records them leaves the correction to a person.
How it works

A closed loop that runs on its own.

NEX combines observation, analysis and control in a single system. The cycle repeats continuously and adapts to what the plant needs at that moment.

Perception

Cameras and sensors continuously measure the state of the crop, the nutrient solution and the air.

  • images
  • EC
  • pH
  • temperature
  • humidity

Decision

The cognitive core recognises species and phenological stage and works out the right strategy, like a tailored agronomic diagnosis.

  • computer vision
  • machine learning
  • agronomic models

Action

NEX directly drives pumps, valves and climate equipment. No manual step between the data and the intervention.

  • dosing
  • irrigation
  • climate

↻ Then the cycle starts again, checking the effect of the intervention.

The platform

Three modules, one decision logic.

Start with the process that weighs most on your costs and add the other modules when you need them. They all share the same data and the same intelligence.

NEX-F

Fertigation

Prepares and corrects the nutrient solution for the crop stage, dosing stock solutions and pH adjusters.

Controls
EC, pH, dosing
NEX-I

Irrigation

Decides when and how much to irrigate from the plants' actual demand, not a fixed timer.

Controls
cycles, volumes, drainage
NEX-C

Climate

Keeps the microclimate in the optimal window for the crop, acting on ventilation, humidification and temperature.

Controls
temperature, RH, VPD

It fits the system you already have.

NEX is designed for retrofit: it connects to existing infrastructure, without rebuilding the greenhouse or changing how you grow.
